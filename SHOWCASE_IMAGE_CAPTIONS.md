## Optional gallery introduction

> **First Signs is a multilingual, mobile-first care-navigation prototype that helps people move from an early health concern to an appropriate facility, referral, and follow-up. It supports five pathways while keeping screening non-diagnostic, roles separate, and public-health reporting privacy-safe.**
