# Prompt for an AI Video Generator — Maya’s Opening Story

Use this prompt in an AI video generator such as Sora, Runway, Veo, Kling, or Pika. Generate the clip **without spoken dialogue, captions, logos, or on-screen text**. Add the First Signs title card later in your video editor so it remains crisp and editable.

---

## Copy-paste prompt

Create a **20-second, cinematic but realistic 16:9 animated explainer opening** for a Bangladesh health-care navigation hackathon project called “First Signs.” The visual tone must be compassionate, hopeful, modern, trustworthy, and restrained—not dramatic, frightening, or clinical.

### Story

Show one continuous story of **Maya**, an adult Bangladeshi garment worker and mother in Khulna. She has had a persistent cough and feels uncertain about what to do next, but do **not** depict severe illness, a diagnosis, hospital emergency, medication, needles, or medical treatment.

### Shot sequence and timing

**0:00–0:05 — Everyday reality**

Early morning in Khulna, Bangladesh. Maya, a working-class Bangladeshi woman in modest everyday clothing, leaves a small home and walks toward a garment factory. She pauses briefly and coughs once into her elbow, then looks concerned at the factory entrance. The scene should communicate that missing work has a real cost, without showing sadness or distress in an exaggerated way.

**0:05–0:10 — The gap**

After work, Maya stands outside a small, clean neighborhood pharmacy. The pharmacy is welcoming but modest. A friendly pharmacy worker gestures kindly, but Maya remains unsure where she should go next. Use visual storytelling: a subtle floating, abstract dotted line that fades before reaching a distant care facility icon, representing a broken path to care. Do not show a prescription, medicine purchase, diagnosis, or doctor consultation.

**0:10–0:16 — A clearer first step**

Maya takes out a smartphone. The phone displays a generic, fictional, text-free health-navigation interface in calm pale blue, white, and teal, with large simple icons only—no readable UI text, no brand logos, no copyrighted app interface. The abstract dotted line becomes a clear, gentle route from the phone to a simple care-facility icon. Maya’s expression shifts from uncertain to calmly reassured.

**0:16–0:20 — Hopeful handoff**

Maya walks with purpose toward a bright, accessible local care facility in daytime. End on a warm wide shot of Maya entering the facility courtyard, with the visual route line resolving into a soft glowing dot. Leave clean negative space in the center or upper-left for a title card added later: “First Signs — Turning warning signs into completed care.”

### Art direction

- Style: premium 3D editorial animation or refined 2.5D motion-graphic illustration; realistic human proportions; subtle claymorphism-inspired depth.
- Palette: pale sky blue, white, muted teal, soft warm sunlight, deep charcoal accents. Avoid red emergency imagery.
- Camera: gentle cinematic tracking shots, slow and steady movement, shallow depth of field, natural transitions that feel like one short film.
- Setting: respectfully recognizable urban Bangladesh; authentic but not stereotyped. Show clean streets, rickshaw or everyday local cues only in the background, and modest non-branded buildings.
- Character: Maya is an adult. Avoid representing a real person or identifiable public figure.
- Accessibility: high contrast, no fast flashes, no strobe effects, no tiny important visual details.
- Audio: no generated voice, dialogue, lyrics, or sound effects. The editor will add voiceover and quiet ambient music separately.
- Output: 1920×1080, 16:9, 24 or 30 fps, approximately 20 seconds.

### Negative prompt / exclusions

No hospital emergency, ambulance, blood, medical examination, medical equipment, diagnosis, prescription, pills, disease labels, tuberculosis labels, warning text, fear-based imagery, crying, death, suffering, child patient, doctor giving advice, fake user-interface text, logos, watermarks, subtitles, captions, distorted hands, duplicated fingers, unreadable text, or rapid cuts.

The story must communicate **uncertainty becoming a clearer path to appropriate care**, not a cure or a medical diagnosis.

## Voiceover for this 20-second clip

Use this over the animation; it replaces the first part of Slide 1 narration:

> Meet Maya, a garment worker and mother in Khulna. She has been coughing for more than two weeks, but missing work means losing income. A nearby pharmacy is easy to reach, yet she still does not know the right next step. First Signs helps turn that uncertainty into a practical path to appropriate care.

## Editing placement

1. Start the full video directly with this animation.
2. At 0:16, fade in a title card in the animation’s negative space: `First Signs` and `Turning warning signs into completed care`.
3. At 0:20, dissolve into presentation Slide 2, **“People can be lost at every handoff.”**
4. Remove the spoken Maya-story section from Slide 1, or use Slide 1 only as a 2–3 second title card after the animation.
5. Keep the total video below five minutes by reducing the presentation introduction accordingly.
